NOMO / downloads Local workspace

Your workspace.
On your machine.

Chat with your agent. Review its work. Keep a signed history and a way back for captured document changes.

Undo here means one effect, not a whole database. Rewinding live tables to a moment ago takes every legitimate write since; an inverse puts back the rows it names and leaves the rest alone. For a managed Postgres restore the receipt is written into your own schema, so you can check it with SQL and without us.

From source to workspace

Two commands.
Your terminal.

Install Node.js 24 or newer and pnpm 10. Extract the source ZIP, open Terminal in the extracted folder, then run: 

pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm tui

On macOS, NOMO.command in that folder also launches the TUI after dependencies are installed.

Connect a model provider before your first conversation. Existing OpenCode TokenRouter settings can be imported with /connect opencode:tokenrouter. Provider availability and usage limits still apply.

Start in the browser.
