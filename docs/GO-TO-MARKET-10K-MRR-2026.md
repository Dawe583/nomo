# VOID: €10k MRR by 2026-12-31

Status: date-bound execution plan, created 2026-09-30.
Owner: founder, with workstream DRIs assigned during Phase 0.
Canonical general plan: docs/GO-TO-MARKET-10K-MRR.md
Canonical tracking issue: https://github.com/Dawe583/void/issues/1

## 1. Target

Reach at least €10,000 of collected recurring monthly revenue in December 2026, with the payment received or contractually collected by 2026-12-31. This is a year-end MRR attainment target, not a forecast and not a claim that the target will be achieved automatically.

The quality gate follows after the deadline: keep the same or higher recurring revenue for January, February, and March 2027, reconcile churn and support load, and confirm that the revenue is not only founder-supplied custom work disguised as a subscription.

If VOID has an existing MRR baseline, subtract it from the new-revenue target. That baseline is currently an explicit unknown and must be recorded in Phase 0 rather than guessed.

## 2. The recommended revenue path

The fastest credible path is eight paid design partners at an average of €1,250 MRR. This is a scoped, recurring evaluation and support offer, not unlimited consulting and not an unsupported hosted-service promise.

Planning scenarios:

| Scenario | Accounts | Price assumption | December MRR | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Design partner first | 8 | €1,250 | €10,000 | Primary path |
| Team volume | 41 | €249 | €10,209 | Secondary, only if self-serve is genuinely repeatable |
| Blended | 20 Team + 4 design partners | €249 + €1,300 | €10,180 | Fallback if demand splits between small teams and deeper pilots |
| Enterprise heavy | 4 | €2,500 | €10,000 | Do not use until hosting, support, identity, data boundaries, and contract scope are real |

The primary offer includes a named workflow, a disposable or customer-controlled evaluation environment, a weekly working session, bounded adapter or policy support, evidence review, and a written outcome. It excludes unlimited integration work, production credentials, autonomous access to customer systems, and any claim that an unfinished adapter is production ready.

The Team €249 price remains a hypothesis. Keep it unchanged until five qualified conversations have tested the scope. Do not change the core safety mechanism from open to paid merely to force the revenue model; charge for organizational operation, support, governance, and service commitments that the team can actually deliver.

## 3. The funnel math

The year-end target needs eight paying accounts. The operating funnel is:

- 240 named ICP accounts by 2026-10-18.
- 120 personalized contacts or warm introductions by 2026-11-01.
- 48 first conversations by 2026-11-22.
- 24 qualified opportunities by 2026-11-29.
- 12 scoped evaluations by 2026-12-06.
- 8 paid design partners with collected recurring revenue by 2026-12-31.

These are operating requirements, not evidence that conversion will happen. If a stage conversion is below the required rate for two consecutive weeks, change the message, channel, ICP, or offer. Do not simply double the volume without diagnosing the loss.

Qualification rule: a prospect is qualified only when they name a consequential agent workflow, a current failure or risk, the evaluator, the disposable evaluation environment, a budget path, and a dated next meeting.

Activation rule: an evaluation is activated only when it reaches one governed hold and independently verifies one signed ledger export outside the VOID runtime.

Revenue rule: MRR is collected recurring revenue in euros. Unpaid pilots, signed-but-not-collected contracts, one-off implementation, grants, and illustrative website numbers do not count.

## 4. ICP and first wedge

Target platform, security, and AI infrastructure teams that already allow agents to write to consequential internal systems. The first workflow must be concrete: database mutation, CRM owner change, storage deletion, support action, financial operation, or another write where a wrong action has a visible consequence.

Avoid these first:

- generic AI enthusiasts without a live agent write;
- broad compliance buyers with no evaluation owner;
- teams asking for a complete hosted platform before the install path is proven;
- integrations selected because they look impressive rather than because a qualified buyer needs them;
- a broad observability or agent analytics position that makes VOID interchangeable with logging.

The message is narrow: VOID puts a policy-checked, fail-closed, signed record in front of agent writes and makes the record independently verifiable. It does not make every action reversible, prove that the agent's reasoning is correct, or substitute for a customer's full security and compliance program.

## 5. Week-by-week execution calendar

### Week 0: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-04

Outcome: the team can state the target, baseline, offer, buyer, and evidence path.

- Record current MRR, active customers, design partners, available founder hours, and engineering capacity. Unknown is a valid value until verified.
- Assign one DRI role each for founder, product, engineering, distribution, sales/research, content, and operations.
- Run the full-history secret scan in Dawe583/void.
- Decide whether the product repository becomes public. Never make it public as an accidental side effect of packaging.
- Choose the package name and release owner.
- Create the evaluation brief and the external-claim review checklist.
- Freeze unrelated roadmap work unless it directly improves install, activation, proof, conversion, or retention.

Exit: a signed-off one-page offer and a board with owner, due date, evidence link, next step, and blocker state for every Phase 0 item.

### Week 1: 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-11

Outcome: the first user path is reproducible and the first account list exists.

- Build the five-minute clean-machine path: install, configure, run one governed hold, export, and verify.
- Produce a sample signed ledger bundle with no customer data.
- Create the first 50 ICP accounts and the first outreach hypotheses.
- Run five discovery conversations using the qualification rule.
- Draft the design-partner scope, price band, payment terms, and stop condition.

Exit: one independent evaluator can follow the path without maintainer intervention, or the exact failure is recorded as the top engineering blocker.

### Week 2: 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-18

Outcome: proof and demand start in parallel.

- Reach 100 named ICP accounts and 10 qualified conversations.
- Reach three scoped evaluations or record why the market is not moving.
- Prepare package metadata and MCP registry fields.
- Publish the first evidence-backed technical artifact: independent verification plus one fail-closed refusal.
- Prepare the first founder post and three small derivatives.

Exit: the same problem shape appears in at least five conversations, or the ICP/wedge is changed in the decisions log.

### Week 3: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-25

Outcome: first money conversation and first activation.

- Reach 150 accounts and 15 qualified conversations.
- Activate the first evaluation: governed hold plus verified export.
- Make the first paid design-partner offer with a written scope.
- Publish the technical post only after claims pass evidence review.
- Create the support runbook for install, policy, hold, export, and verification.

Exit: one buyer has accepted a payment path or the offer has a specific recorded objection that changes scope or price.

### Week 4: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-01

Outcome: first paid account and a repeatable onboarding path.

- Reach 200 accounts and 20 qualified conversations.
- Close and collect the first paid design partner.
- Activate the second evaluation.
- Prepare the release candidate, but do not claim a public release until CI, artifacts, credentials, and real-service evidence are complete.
- Review whether the current €1,000 to €1,500 design-partner band matches the scope delivered.

### Week 5: 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-08

Outcome: two paid accounts and the first reusable proof package.

- Reach 24 qualified conversations and four active evaluations.
- Close the second paid design partner.
- Publish a proof package: quickstart, sample ledger, verifier output, failure case, limits, and contact path.
- Start a weekly customer review with explicit support load.

Exit: two paying accounts have the same or adjacent success criteria and no hidden production access was needed to demonstrate value.

### Week 6: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-15

Outcome: three paid accounts and a focused product backlog.

- Close the third paid design partner.
- Convert the top three objections into one product task, one documentation task, and one sales clarification.
- Run the first price and packaging review.
- Remove any backlog item that is not tied to install, activation, proof, conversion, or retention.

Exit: the team knows which work is repeated support and which work is product leverage.

### Week 7: 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22

Outcome: four paid accounts and a functioning referral loop.

- Close the fourth paid design partner.
- Ask activated accounts for one relevant introduction only after delivering their agreed evidence.
- Reach 48 first conversations cumulatively.
- Publish a comparison of ordinary logs, guardrails, and governed writes without attacking unsupported alternatives.

Exit: at least one introduction or referral is based on a real customer workflow, not a generic request to share the product.

### Week 8: 2026-11-23 to 2026-11-29

Outcome: five paid accounts and a conversion diagnosis.

- Close the fifth paid design partner.
- Reach 24 qualified opportunities cumulatively.
- Review losses by category: no urgency, no authority, no budget, no install, no trust, or no current agent write.

### Week 9: 2026-11-30 to 2026-12-06

Outcome: six paid accounts and 12 scoped evaluations.

- Close the sixth paid design partner.
- Reach 12 scoped evaluations cumulatively.
- Make the customer-facing evidence bundle self-serve where possible.

### Week 10: 2026-12-07 to 2026-12-13

Outcome: seven paid accounts and no critical activation blocker.

- Close the seventh paid design partner.
- Fix only blockers that prevent a qualified account from paying or activating.
- Confirm January renewal dates, support commitments, and customer owners for every account.
- Prepare a year-end customer and revenue review with no illustrative numbers.

Exit: the eighth account has a named target, a next meeting, a scope, and a payment path.

### Week 11: 2026-12-14 to 2026-12-20

Outcome: eight accounts are closed or the gap is explicit.

- Close the eighth paid design partner and collect recurring revenue.
- If the primary path is short, use the approved blended fallback only where the delivered scope is real.
- Do not sell unsupported hosting or production guarantees to close the gap.
- Freeze new feature work unless it unblocks a signed and payable account.

Exit: collected December recurring revenue is at or above €10,000, or the remaining gap has a written recovery plan and a decision to stop claiming the target.

### Week 12: 2026-12-21 to 2026-12-31

Outcome: year-end reconciliation and truth.

- Reconcile every recurring payment to an account, scope, owner, start date, and renewal date.
- Separate MRR from one-off setup, support overages, and unpaid commitments.
- Record December MRR, customer count, churn risk, support hours, and gross founder time.
- Publish an internal result as achieved, missed, or partially achieved. Do not round up.
- Start the Q1 three-month retention and renewal gate.

Exit: the result is auditable from source records and the next quarter has one evidence-backed focus.

## 6. Product and engineering plan

### Release path

1. Confirm the supported install shape.
2. Build the CLI or MCP artifact.
3. Add package metadata and registry manifest.
4. Provide a bootstrap configuration and starter policy.
5. Run the first governed hold.
6. Export a signed ledger bundle.
7. Verify the export outside the runtime.
8. Test the supported real-service path where credentials and evidence exist.
9. Run the secret scan and release checks.
10. Publish only after the visibility and release decisions are explicit.

### Engineering priorities

- P0: clean-machine install and first governed hold.
- P0: signed export and standalone verifier.
- P0: failure behavior for unknown or unsupported actions.
- P1: package and MCP registry readiness.
- P1: onboarding and support diagnostics.
- P1: one integration pulled by a qualified paid evaluation.
- P2: scale, hosted service, broad adapter coverage, and platform expansion.

The team must not build a hosted multi-tenant service, SSO, KMS, enterprise SLA, or broad connector matrix merely because a prospect mentions it. Add each only after a qualified account supplies a scope, payment path, data boundary, and acceptance test.

## 7. Sales and research plan

Daily sales block:

- 30 minutes: research five accounts.
- 45 minutes: send or prepare personalized outreach.
- 30 minutes: follow up on active opportunities.
- 30 minutes: write evidence notes and the next dated step.

Weekly sales outputs:

- 20 new named accounts until 240 are reached.
- 10 personalized contacts until 120 are reached.
- 4 qualified conversations per week after Week 2.
- 1 scoped evaluation per week after Week 3.
- 1 payment conversation per week after the first activation.

Every call records the workflow, consequence, current workaround, buyer, evaluator, environment, budget path, next date, and evidence status. A positive comment without a next date is not pipeline.

## 8. Content and distribution plan

One primary proof artifact per week:

1. Independently verified signed ledger bundle.
2. Fail-closed refusal for unknown or unsupported action.
3. Exact inverse versus explicit non-inverse case.
4. Comparison of logs, guardrails, and governed writes.
5. Design-partner evaluation guide.
6. Customer-approved workflow result, only if approval exists.

Each primary artifact produces three derivatives: a short founder post, a terminal or diagram capture, and one direct question for the target buyer. Every claim links to evidence or is labeled illustrative.

Distribution order:

1. GitHub artifact and quickstart.
2. Founder technical post.
3. Direct outreach to matched accounts.
4. Community discussion with the reproducible artifact.
5. MCP Registry or other public distribution after release gates.
6. Show HN or equivalent launch post only when the artifact is genuinely installable.

No launch post substitutes for a working install or a customer conversation.

## 11. Contingency plans

### If no qualified demand by 2026-10-18

Do not increase volume first. Review the ICP, workflow consequence, message, and proof artifact. Interview five more people from a different but adjacent buyer role and record the change in #decisions.

### If evaluations start but do not activate

Stop selling additional scope for one week. Fix the clean-machine path, policy bootstrap, first hold, or verifier. Activation failure is a product problem until proven otherwise.

### If buyers want integration work but will not pay recurring

Offer a paid bounded evaluation only if it has a recurring continuation, a measurable outcome, and a stop condition. Otherwise classify it as consulting demand, not MRR evidence.

### If the primary design-partner path is below four paid accounts by 2026-11-29

Run the approved blended model only with real delivered scope. Test Team at €249 with a narrow standard path, but do not claim product-market fit from a handful of discounted or founder-funded accounts.

### If €10k is missed on 2026-12-31

Publish the exact result internally, preserve the evidence, and choose one of three actions: extend the same wedge with a measured gap, change the offer, or stop the program. Do not hide the miss by counting signed-but-unpaid contracts.

## 12. Success criteria

The target is achieved on 2026-12-31 only if:

- collected December recurring revenue is at least €10,000;
- each included account has a recurring scope and source record;
- no one-off work is counted as MRR;
- the customer and support owner are known;
- the product evidence for the sold scope is stored;
- the result can be independently reconciled from source records.

The following quarter then measures whether the revenue survives contact with renewals, support cost, and actual product value.
