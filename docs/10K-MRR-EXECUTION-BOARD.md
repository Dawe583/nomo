This is the operating board, not a revenue forecast. A row is complete only when its evidence link exists and another person can reproduce or inspect the result.

## 1. North-star outcome

Reach **at least €10,000 of collected recurring monthly revenue in December 2026**, preferably through **eight scoped design partners at an average of €1,250 MRR**, then hold or exceed that level through January–March 2027. Do not count unpaid commitments, grants, one-off work, or illustrative website prices.

The company is not allowed to trade away product truth for the target. VOID must continue to distinguish:

- integrity from correctness;
- an inverse from a promise of universal reversibility;
- fixture evidence from production certification;
- a signed contract from collected MRR;
- a customer request from a qualified opportunity.

## 6. Evidence gates

A claim may move from internal draft to public content only after the relevant gate passes:

1. **Install gate:** clean machine, documented prerequisites, exact command, retained output.
2. **Safety gate:** unsupported/unknown path refuses or clearly states its limit.
3. **Ledger gate:** signed export verifies outside the runtime.
4. **Provider gate:** real-service evidence is separated from fixture evidence.
5. **Commercial gate:** price is labelled hypothesis until repeated WTP signals exist.
6. **Customer gate:** logo, quote, result, or workflow is approved by the customer and publication owner.
7. **Release gate:** visibility, secrets, artifact digest, rollback, and owner are explicit.

If a gate is missing, the wording must say `illustrative`, `fixture evidence`, `beta`, or `roadmap` as appropriate.

## 7. Content production system

Each weekly proof package contains one source artifact and exactly three derivatives:

- source document or reproducible repository artifact;
- short founder post with one claim and one proof;
- terminal capture or diagram;
- one direct buyer question.

Six-week sequence:

| Week | Primary proof | Buyer question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Signed ledger bundle independently verified | What must an approver prove after an agent write? |
| 2 | Fail-closed unknown action | Which agent action must never silently pass? |
| 3 | Exact inverse vs explicit non-inverse | Where is recovery real, and where should the system refuse? |
| 4 | Logs vs guardrails vs governed writes | What does your current log fail to prove? |
| 5 | Design-partner evaluation guide | Can your team test this in a disposable environment? |
| 6 | Approved customer workflow result | What evidence would make this safe to adopt? |

## 8. Stop rules and escalation

Stop or re-scope the sale when any of these is true:

- the team cannot state the success evidence or data boundary;
- the requested work is unlimited integration/consulting without recurring scope;
- production credentials are requested before the supported path is proven;
- a buyer wants a hosted, SSO, KMS, SLA, or compliance promise that has no evidence gate;
- five evaluations cannot reach activation;
- twenty qualified conversations do not produce five with the same urgent problem.

Escalation format in Slack:

```text
ESCALATION
Owner: @person / role
Decision needed by: YYYY-MM-DD
Problem: one sentence
Impact: revenue / activation / safety / release
Options: A / B / C
Recommendation: one option
Evidence: GitHub or artifact link
If no decision: exact consequence
```

## 9. Canonical record rules

- GitHub documents and issues hold durable product and strategy truth.
- The workboard holds account, funnel, and payment source records.
- Slack holds coordination, decisions, evidence pointers, and reminders.
- Slack never holds secrets, customer credentials, private keys, or database connection strings.
- A Slack update without an owner, status, due date, evidence, and next step is an observation, not a task.
- Any decision in Slack that survives the week must be copied to `#decisions` and the linked GitHub issue.
