## Distribution and retained-format rules

Mac App Store, notarized direct Mac download and native iOS/iPadOS are three independent distribution tracks. Public managed cloud is not a first-beta executor. Mac bundle identifier `dev.void.desktop` and version `0.1.0` are development metadata, not approved store identity. `void.adapter-capability.v1` describes scope and does not authorize execution. Preserve `certification:false` on Git/documents and the uncertified filesystem profile until a separate certification process exists. Sources: `packages/connectors/src/capability.ts`, `packages/cli/src/conformance.ts`.

Journal `void.operation.v1` and `void.recovery-job.v1`, encrypted vault SQLite and legacy files, JSONL signatures, encryption/signing keys and document storage form one restore set. No general 1.x compatibility or downgrade promise until old-build fixtures prove it. A wrong key or truncated/unanchored history must be reported as unverifiable, not silently repaired. Sources: `packages/runtime/src/journal.ts`, `packages/runtime/src/reserved-vault.ts`, `docs/RECOVERY-CONTRACT.md`.

## Blocking inputs

Full Xcode and iOS SDK; Mac App Store team/App ID/provisioning/signing and separate Developer ID; physical iPhone/iPad and clean test Mac; support/legal/privacy owner; bounded-cost provider sandbox; local-network scope and approved design direction. Do not replace these with fixtures. Store uploads, public release and purchases require separate explicit approval.
