## Decision summary

- First outcome hypothesis: The $15-an-Hour Expert, as a hypothesis to test.
- Later capability: Own Your Intelligence, after repeated use and expert-reviewed corrections.
- Optional deployment tier: AI for the Air-Gapped Economy, only after written demand and funding.
- Current access: one founder-reported CFO/owner contact. No completed interview, workflow, sample permission, paid pilot, or market demand yet. The contact is not a segment decision.
- Commercial caution: LeadsPicker data does not by itself grant permission for direct marketing. Verify vendor terms, provenance, lawful basis, and country/channel rules before outreach.
- Build gate: no customer product code until a specific workflow, user, expert, buyer, permitted evidence, and paid-pilot path are identified. Continue NOMO first-live work independently.

## Evidence standard

Each market claim must point to dated primary evidence. Each product decision must point to customer behavior or a named experiment. Keep vendor claims distinct from independent verification. Revisit fast-changing law, product availability, and market statistics before external use.
